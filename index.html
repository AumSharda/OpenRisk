Why OpenRisk exists

Less uncertainty.
More possibility.

Building a business means making decisions without always knowing what's around the corner. We believe understanding risk shouldn't be a privilege reserved for companies with big budgets and specialist teams.

OpenRisk is a free, approachable place to explore those decisions, understand what deserves attention, and work out which questions to ask next. No jargon, scare tactics, or sales pitch.

Our goal isn't to eliminate risk. It's to help people understand it, prepare thoughtfully, and keep building.

💚

Calm over fear

We aim to explain uncertainty without amplifying anxiety or making promises we can't support.

🔎

Clarity over jargon

Practical questions, everyday language, and transparent limitations.

🚀

Progress over perfection

Small, useful next steps so you can keep doing the work that matters.

What OpenRisk is — and isn't. OpenRisk is an educational decision-exploration tool, not an insurance broker, lawyer, accountant, financial adviser, or emergency service. It does not assess your coverage, determine legal obligations, or guarantee that a decision is safe. Requirements vary by contract, location, business structure, and policy wording. For decisions with material consequences, check the actual documents and seek qualified local advice. No data you enter is sent to an OpenRisk server by this static website.
